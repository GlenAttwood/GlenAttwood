# G'day, I'm Glen 👋

Owner and resident developer at **[Hearns Hobbies](https://www.hearnshobbies.com.au)**, one of Melbourne's oldest hobby stores, right on Flinders Street.

By day I build the software that runs the shop (and, honestly, most nights too).

## 🛠️ What I build

- **Retail POS and inventory system**: a custom VB.NET point-of-sale and stock system that's been growing with the business for years
- **E-commerce integrations**: syncing products, stock and orders between the store and Shopify
- **Shipping automation**: tying order fulfilment into StarShipIt
- **AI tools for retail**: experimenting with AI for reorder suggestions, pricing and product data quality
